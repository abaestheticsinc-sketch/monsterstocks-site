Frequently Asked Questions

How do monsters evolve?

Monsters evolve based on your Bond Journey — the combination of days held, price performance, and dividend/earnings events. Keep holding your stocks and watch your monsters grow.

What is the Arena?

The Arena is a weekly competition where Trainers pick which stock will perform best for the week. Picks lock at market open Monday. Results are tallied at Friday close.

How do I cancel my Trainer subscription?

Go to Profile → Trainer ✓ → Tap to manage subscription. This opens the App Store subscription manager where you can cancel, pause, or change your plan.

My brokerage connection isn't syncing

Go to Profile → Brokerage → Reconnect. If the issue persists, disconnect and reconnect. We read portfolio data via SnapTrade and cannot execute trades on your behalf.

I found a bug

Email support@monsterstocks.app with a description of what happened and your device/iOS version. Screenshots are helpful.
